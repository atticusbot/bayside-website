The Lankford Hotel is located at 807 North Atlantic Avenue in Ocean City, Maryland — one of the most historic properties on the Maryland shore. Situated directly on the Ocean City Boardwalk at 8th Street, the hotel offers guests immediate access to the Atlantic Ocean beach, local dining, shops, and entertainment. Ocean City is the most visited destination in Maryland, welcoming eight million visitors each summer. The Lankford is ideal for guests who want history, authenticity, and an unbeatable boardwalk location.
