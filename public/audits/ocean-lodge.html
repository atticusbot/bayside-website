[ 01 ] Where Things Stand

Ocean Lodge sits at 2.8★ across 343 Google reviews with zero owner responses on record. Most travelers filter hotels below 3.5 stars — which means the property is invisible to the majority of guests before they ever read a single review. Competing properties on the boardwalk average 4.3–4.5 stars.

At the same time, the Ocean Lodge brand has real strengths worth building on: three distinct properties under one roof — North, South, and Mallard Apartments — two blocks from the boardwalk, kitchen amenities, and a loyal repeat-guest base that isn't being captured or nurtured. The gap between where this brand is and where it could be is an opportunity.

This proposal covers everything we'll build together: reputation recovery, a new website, email automation, a content engine, and a full ad campaign — the complete digital infrastructure for a competitive independent hotel in Ocean City.

[ 02 ] What We're Building
01 Google Business Profile Overhaul Setup + Ongoing

Your Google profile is the first thing a potential guest sees. Right now it's incomplete and silent. We rebuild it from the ground up and keep it active.

View details
  • 25–30 owner-uploaded photos integrated from the professional shoot (exterior, rooms, amenities, boardwalk proximity)
  • Property description built around your strongest assets: 2 blocks from the boardwalk, kitchen amenities, value pricing, direct booking advantage
  • All amenity tags, hours, policies, direct booking link confirmed and accurate
  • Q&A section seeded with common guest questions
  • Review responses — every review from the past 12 months responded to, plus all new reviews on an ongoing basis. Drafted by us, approved by you before posting.
On review responses: We go back one full year — recent enough to matter, targeted enough to look human. All new reviews get a response within 48 hours on an ongoing basis. Responses are written individually; no templates.
02 Professional Photography One-Time

Ocean Lodge currently has zero owner-uploaded photos on Google. Travelers make booking decisions on visuals first. A single professional shoot produces assets that work across every channel — Google, the new website, OTA listings, Instagram — for years.

View details
  • Full property shoot: exterior, lobby, room types (North + South + Mallard), amenities, parking, views
  • Boardwalk and location context shots — lean into the 2-block proximity advantage
  • Lifestyle shots for social and ad creative
  • Delivered as an organized asset library ready to deploy across all channels
03 New Website Setup + Ongoing

A fresh, standalone website for the Ocean Lodge brand — minimal, beach-kitschy cozy, nothing like the generic OC hotel template. Built for direct bookings, optimized for Google and AI search, and structured to carry the full brand family.

View details
  • Brand architecture: Ocean Lodge North, Ocean Lodge South, and Mallard Apartments nested under one domain with distinct pages — plus a "sister property" link to Surf Inn Suites
  • Direct booking: Prominent, unavoidable booking link with a visible discount for booking direct vs. OTA ("Book direct. Best rate guaranteed.")
  • Crystal-clear policies: Dedicated policies page addressing parking, deposits, check-in/out, and cancellation — the exact sources of contention in current reviews. No more surprises.
  • SEO foundation: Proper on-page structure, fast load times, mobile-first, Core Web Vitals optimized
  • GEO-ready structure: Hotel schema markup, FAQ pages with structured Q&A (feeds directly into Google AI Overviews and ChatGPT citations), consistent NAP across every page
  • Design direction: Fresh and minimal with beach-kitschy warmth — warm sand tones, ocean references, handwritten-feel accents. Feels like a place worth staying, not a booking engine.
04 Email Automation Setup + Ongoing

Three automated emails that do real work: reduce pre-arrival misunderstandings, make guests feel welcomed on arrival, and turn happy guests into reviewers at exactly the right moment.

View details
  • Booking confirmation: Personalized, warm, practical. Explicitly covers parking policy, deposit terms, and check-in process — the exact issues generating bad reviews today. Sets accurate expectations before guests arrive.
  • Day of arrival: Welcome message with check-in details, local recommendations, and a reminder of direct booking perks for their next visit
  • Post-departure: Thank you note with a direct ask for a Google, Booking.com, or TripAdvisor review — with a discount code for booking direct on their next stay. Sent 24 hours after checkout while the experience is fresh.
The post-departure email is the engine behind review volume growth. A consistent, well-timed ask to every guest — automated — is how you go from 5 reviews a month to 20+.
05 Content Engine Monthly

Consistent social and blog content that keeps Ocean Lodge visible and alive online year-round — not just in season. Planned, written, and posted by us.

View details
  • Social media (Instagram + Facebook): 3 posts per week — mix of property photos, local OC content, guest spotlights, seasonal promotions, and direct booking reminders
  • Blog (2 posts/month): SEO-targeted articles that answer real travel questions ("Best oceanfront hotels in Ocean City," "What to do in OC with kids," "Hotels near the Ocean City boardwalk") — drives organic search traffic and feeds AI search citations
  • All content reviewed and approved by you before publishing
06 Google & Instagram Ad Campaign Monthly

A managed ad campaign running through the full summer season — the period where every dollar of visibility translates directly to bookings.

View details
  • Google Search Ads: Target high-intent searches ("hotels Ocean City MD," "ocean city boardwalk hotels," "ocean city vacation rentals") and capture travelers actively looking to book
  • Instagram / Meta Ads: Visual campaigns using professional photography — retargeting website visitors and reaching new audiences in feeder markets (DC, Philadelphia, Baltimore, Pittsburgh)
  • Monthly reporting: impressions, clicks, booking conversions, cost per acquisition
  • Campaign creative produced by us; strategy adjusted monthly based on performance data
Ad spend is separate from the management fee and billed directly to your account. We recommend $600–1,000/month during peak season (May–September). We manage the budget allocation, targeting, and optimization.
07 Monthly Performance Reports Monthly

Every month, a clear picture of where you stand and what's moving.

View details
  • Review volume + star rating trend (Google, TripAdvisor, Booking.com, Expedia)
  • Competitive benchmarking vs. 3 comparable OC properties

  • Email sequence performance (open rates, click rates, review conversion)

  • Ad campaign performance: spend, impressions, bookings attributed
